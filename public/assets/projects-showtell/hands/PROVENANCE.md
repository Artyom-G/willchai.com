# Hand model sources

The hand meshes come from the WebXR Input Profiles assets package. Upstream commit: `c5356912d0c3bc43c6db8aa3926d8951a7716449`.

Source: https://github.com/immersive-web/webxr-input-profiles/tree/c5356912d0c3bc43c6db8aa3926d8951a7716449/packages/assets/profiles/generic-hand

The assets retain their original geometry and skin weights. TACHYBOARD constructs anatomical bone chains and joint limits at runtime. The assets package MIT notice is included in `LICENSE.txt`. Three.js 0.186.0 uses the MIT notice in `THREE-LICENSE.txt`.
